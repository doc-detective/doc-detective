Drag a widget and scroll the page

  1. Go to the drag and drop page.
  2. Drag Table onto Drop Zone 1.
  3. Check that Table (dropped) appears in the drop zone.
  4. Press and hold Chart for one second.
  5. Swipe up to scroll toward the bottom of the page.
  6. Swipe down a third of the screen to scroll back.
  7. Swipe from the middle of the page toward the top.
