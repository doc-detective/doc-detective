# Open a built-in app

## Windows: Character Map

1. Open Character Map, and wait for its Select button.
2. Click **Select**.
3. Check that the window shows the **Copy** button.
4. Check the Copy button again, picking the window by its title, by name, and as the newest window.
5. Close Character Map.

## macOS: Calculator

1. Open Calculator with `SESSIONS_DOCS=1` in its environment, and wait for its `1` button.
2. In the first window, press the `1` button.
3. Close Calculator.

## Android: Settings

1. Open the Settings app, and wait for the Network & internet entry.
2. Tap the Network & internet entry.
3. Check that the screen shows **Internet**.
4. Close the Settings app.

## Android: your own app

1. Install `build/sessions-demo.apk` on a headless Android 14 phone emulator named `sessions-pixel`, open its main activity, and wait for the Welcome screen.
2. Close the app.

## iOS: Settings

1. On the iPhone simulator named `sessions-iphone`, open the Settings app, and wait for the General entry.
2. Tap the General entry.
3. Check that the screen shows **About**.
4. Close the Settings app.
